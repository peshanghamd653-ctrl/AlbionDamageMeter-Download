# Albion Damage Meter

A free damage and healing meter for Albion Online, with a crafting tab for market prices and crafting plans.
Windows only.

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)** — one file, `AlbionDamageMeter.exe`. Nothing to install.

## Running it

1. **Right-click the file and choose "Run as administrator."** It needs that to read your game's network traffic.
2. Windows will say **"Windows protected your PC."** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. That warning appears
   because the file isn't signed with a paid certificate, not because anything is wrong with it. Your antivirus may
   ask too, for the same reason.
3. **Open the meter before you log in.** Started mid-game, it can't see the players who were already around you, and
   it says so in the window.

## What it shows

- **Meter:** damage, healing and damage taken, per fight, for you, your party, your guild and alliance, or everyone
  nearby. Click a name for the breakdown by ability.
- **Crafting → Prices:** search any item and see the cheapest seller and best buyer in every royal city, Caerleon,
  Brecilien and the Black Market, in every quality and enchantment, with where each price came from and how old it is.
- **Crafting → Plans:** tell it your city, your silver and how fast you want the money, and it ranks what you can
  craft, tells you which prices to confirm in the game's market, and then shows the plan: shopping list, steps and the
  full money breakdown, including returned materials, station fees and market tax.

## How it works, and what it doesn't do

- It **reads network traffic only**, the same way other meters and the price tools do. It never touches, reads or
  changes the game itself.
- **No overlay and no clicking through it.** It's a normal window you place next to the game.
- **Your own game, your own PC.** Prices the meter sees are not uploaded anywhere.
- It keeps the game data of your **last 5 sessions** on your PC so problems can be looked into. That can be switched
  off in the settings (the gear icon). Those files hold other players' names and chat, so don't pass them around.
- The crafting tab uses the internet for the community item list (ao-bin-dumps) and the public price website
  (Albion Online Data Project).
